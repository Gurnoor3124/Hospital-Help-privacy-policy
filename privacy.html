Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2025

Introduction

We value your trust. This Privacy Policy explains how Hospital Help handles information when you use our services.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal details such as your name, email, or contact information. The only data we process is your IP address.

How We Use This Information

Your IP address is temporarily processed to determine your approximate location. This allows our app to provide helpful location‑based features — for example, helping you find hospitals near you in a click of a button. We do not store, share, or sell this information.

Children’s Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since only IP addresses are processed, no identifying data is retained.

Data Retention

IP addresses are processed only at the time of use and are not stored in our systems.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the limited information we process. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: HDGRAapps@gmail.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Effective date” above.
